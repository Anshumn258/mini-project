About Techno Utsav

Techno Utsav is the premier inter-collegiate technical fest hosted annually at Saket College, designed to inspire, entertain, and bring out the best technical minds.

🎓 Saket College Pride

Hosted at Saket College, Techno Utsav brings together students from all across the region to celebrate technology, innovation, and academic excellence in an energetic campus atmosphere.

💻 Driven by IT & CS Students

Organized passionately by the Information Technology and Computer Science departments, our student committee works tirelessly to craft challenging competitions and memorable experiences.

🚀 Fun Meets Technology

It's not just about complex code and logic! We combine intense technical hackathons with exciting gaming arenas, fun side-quests, workshops, and entertainment to make tech accessible to everyone.
